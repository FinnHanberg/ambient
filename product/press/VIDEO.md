# Short films — concepts

Rules for all of them: monochrome, real UI only, no stock footage, no voiceover
explaining what you can already see, no music that has to carry the idea. Screen
recording at 60fps, cursor visible, no zoom-punches faster than 400ms.

---

## 01 — "The ring" · 8s · the one that travels

The whole product in one gesture, with no setup.

| t | frame |
|---|---|
| 0.0 | A website, still. Cursor drifts to a logo. |
| 0.8 | Audio: *"increase the opacity of this"* — the word "this" lands. |
| 1.0 | Freeze. The ring draws on at the cursor, 200ms. |
| 1.6 | The frame lifts out of the page and lands as a thumbnail in a note row. |
| 3.0 | Two more notes drop in beneath it, same motion, faster. |
| 5.5 | The review panel settles. Cursor hits Copy. |
| 7.0 | Cut to black. Wordmark. |

Cut for autoplay without sound — the ring has to read silently.

---

## 02 — "One hold" · 18s · the format demo

A real pass across a real page, uncut, in one take.

Single continuous screen recording. A small `⌃ fn` indicator sits bottom-left, lit
throughout, so the single hold is legible. The note counter climbs 1 → 7 as the cursor
moves down the page. No cuts, no speed ramps. The honesty of the unbroken take is the
argument.

End on the review panel with seven notes. Total elapsed time on screen.

---

## 03 — "Move this above that" · 6s · the differentiator

The narrowest possible film, made for a reply in a thread.

One sentence, two pointing words, two rings landing at different moments on different
elements. Slow enough to read the timing: ring one on "this", ring two on "that". The
two crops fan out side by side. Cut.

This is the shot that answers "how is this different from a screenshot tool".

---

## 04 — "Nothing leaves" · 10s · the objection

For the privacy question, which will be the top comment.

Airplane mode toggled on, visibly, in the menu bar. A full pass runs anyway. The
review panel fills. Cut to the shots folder: three files, nothing else.

No claims on screen beyond what the recording shows.
