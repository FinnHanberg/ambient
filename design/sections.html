Turn off your network. It still works.

Speech

Costs you: every sentence here is a promise you can be audited on. Say “nothing is uploaded” only while it stays literally true.

12

The permissions, before you download

Microphone

To hear you while the keys are held. Without it: nothing works.

Screen Recording

To crop what you pointed at. Without it: notes arrive with no picture.

Accessibility

To read the element under the cursor — and to see the hotkey at all. Without it: the key does nothing, silently.

That last one is the trap. A global hotkey is invisible without Accessibility, so the app looks dead rather than unpermitted. Ambient checks on launch, says so on the panel, and clears itself the moment you grant it.

Costs you: nothing, and it is the highest-leverage section on the page. Stage two of the funnel is where this product loses people.

13

What it gets wrong

Transcription drifts

Product names and strong accents come back wrong often enough to matter. Every note is editable before it sends.

Some apps say nothing

Chromium and Electron expose no element tree unless a screen reader runs. There you get the crop and the URL, not the element.

macOS only

It is built on frameworks that exist nowhere else. There is no Windows version and there isn't going to be one.

Costs you: a competitor can quote this page back at you. Worth it — sophisticated buyers discount any page without it.

Who this isn't for

Skip it if

  • — You review in a browser and file tickets there already
  • — Your feedback is one line at a time, not twenty
  • — You share an office and can just say it out loud

It earns its place if

  • — You hand UI work to an agent and re-describe the same screen daily
  • — Your reviews span several apps, not one browser tab
  • — You have said “no, the other one” more than once this week

Costs you: some traffic that would have converted and churned. That is the point — disqualification raises conversion among the people who stay.

20

The four questions people actually ask

Is it listening all the time?

No. Audio reaches the recogniser only while both keys are down. Let go and it stops — not muted, stopped.

What happens to the screenshots?

They sit in a folder on your Mac. Frames that no note claimed are discarded when the pass ends.

Do I need an API key?

No. Everything runs on the model built into macOS. A key is optional and only changes how the notes are worded.

Why is it free?

Because it costs nothing to run — no server, no inference bill. I built it for my own work and there is no reason to charge you for a copy.

Costs you: the last answer commits you to free. Say it only if you mean it — reversing it later reads as a bait.

Part three

Ten arguments about time, none of them the same argument.

“Saves you time” is the weakest version of this claim because everyone makes it. Each block below makes it differently — as physics, as arithmetic you can check, as a calendar, and once by dropping the claim entirely for a better one.
