Every value here is read out of Panel.swift. Drag one and the whole sheet moves with it — the components hold no numbers of their own.

Contract

A component is a canvas draw(x, W, H, p, scale) reached as a material string, exactly like a bitmap. It crops, takes the bar, goes under the optic and exports with no special case.
